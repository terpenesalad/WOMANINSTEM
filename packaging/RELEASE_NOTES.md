## WOMANINSTEM for Windows (64-bit)

**Download one of these:**
- `WOMANINSTEM-x.y.z-setup.exe`: installer with Start Menu / desktop shortcuts and "Open with WOMANINSTEM" for MP3/FLAC.
- `WOMANINSTEM-x.y.z-windows-x64.zip`: portable. Unzip anywhere and run `WOMANINSTEM.exe`.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

### What it does
- Splits any MP3 / FLAC / WAV / OGG into **drums, bass, guitar, keys/piano, lead vocals, backing vocals and "other"** (strings, horns, synths) using Meta's Demucs v4 AI model, running locally on your CPU.
- Lets you plug in your **bass, guitar or mic** through your USB audio interface (ASIO supported) and play along through a full rig: noise gate, compressor, drive pedals, 6 amp models **plus Neural Amp Modeler (.nam) captures**, speaker cabs **plus impulse responses**, studio EQ, chorus, delay, reverb and a tuner.
- Practice tools: mute/solo any stem, loop sections, slow down without pitch change, transpose, and record yourself (mix + rig + dry DI).

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, a CPU with AVX2 (most PCs from 2015 on), 8 GB RAM recommended.
