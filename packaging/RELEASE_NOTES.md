## WOMANINSTEM 2.0 for Windows (64-bit): now with a full recording Studio

**Download one of these:**
- `WOMANINSTEM-x.y.z-setup.exe`: installer with Start Menu / desktop shortcuts, "Open with WOMANINSTEM" for MP3/FLAC, and double-click to open Studio songs (`.wisproj`).
- `WOMANINSTEM-x.y.z-windows-x64.zip`: portable. Unzip anywhere and run `WOMANINSTEM.exe`.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

### New in 2.0: the Studio
Click **STUDIO** at the top of the window. Everything from Play Along is still there, one click away.

- **Software instruments** played from a USB MIDI keyboard or your computer keyboard (*Musical Typing*, `Ctrl+K`): a built-in **Sound Library of 287 instruments and 13 drum kits** (pianos, organs, guitars, basses, strings, choirs, brass, woodwinds, synths), any `.sf2` SoundFont, and the **Studio Synth** (16 presets).
- **Drummer**: 18 grooves (rock, pop, dance, funk, hip hop, ballad, blues, jazz, reggae, bossa nova, metal...) with fills, editable note by note.
- **Record** audio and MIDI on multiple tracks at once, with count-in, metronome, latency compensation, cycle recording and **takes**.
- **Arrange and edit**: move, copy, trim, split, fades, duplicate, copy/paste, snapping, unlimited undo. **Piano roll** with velocity, quantize and drum names. Volume / pan automation.
- **Mix**: channel strips with insert effects, meters, mute/solo, master bus. 11 built-in effects (EQ with live curve, compressor, limiter, gate, reverb, delay, chorus, phaser, tremolo, saturator) and your whole **Amp & Pedals** rig as a plugin on any track.
- **VST3 plugins** with crash-safe scanning.
- **Open in Studio** from Play Along: each stem on its own track, **tempo detected** and lined up to the bar grid, plus an armed track with your current rig to record a cover.
- **Export** mix or stems as WAV / FLAC / OGG, or a MIDI file. Import audio and MIDI files by dragging them in.
- Songs autosave every 2 minutes, with recovery after a crash.

### Play Along (as before)
- Splits any MP3 / FLAC / WAV / OGG into **drums, bass, guitar, keys/piano, lead vocals, backing vocals and "other"** using Meta's Demucs v4 AI model, locally on your CPU.
- Play along with your **bass, guitar or mic** through a full rig: noise gate, compressor, drive pedals, 6 amp models **plus Neural Amp Modeler (.nam) captures**, speaker cabs **plus impulse responses**, studio EQ, chorus, delay, reverb and a tuner.
- Practice tools: mute/solo any stem, loop sections, slow down without pitch change, transpose, record yourself (mix + rig + dry DI).

Requirements: Windows 10/11 64-bit, a CPU with AVX2 (most PCs from 2015 on), 8 GB RAM recommended.
